# Design: build-enrollment-tokens-screen

## Context

G3 item 33 (`docs/g3-plan.md`, row 33), sub-change 33b under the coordinator's split (`brief-33-plan.md`, Niki, 2026-11-04). Its base is `dev` `3191c262` (#447) with 33a `build-workers-screen` ([archive](/openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-04-build-workers-screen/design.md)) and change 1 of the item, `serve-workers-and-enrollment-tokens` (#445), which serves the four token operations of `control-plane.openapi.json` 0.5.0. Every error body is the kernel `Failure` with its reason in `details.reason`; the role is `installation-owner` for all four; the three mutations take the session's CSRF token (`X-CSRF-Token`) and document a 403 of two bodies, `role-required` and `csrf-token-invalid` ("nothing took effect"). `listEnrollmentTokens` (`GET /enrollment-tokens`, query `after`, no `limit`) answers `EnrollmentTokenPage` (`tokens[]` of at most 100, `next` absent on the last page) of the pending tokens in identifier order; `issueEnrollmentToken` (`POST /enrollment-tokens`, body `HostConstraints`, every field optional, `{}` valid) and `rotateEnrollmentToken` (`POST /enrollment-tokens/{tokenId}/rotation`, no body) answer 201 `IssuedEnrollmentToken` (`token` and `secret` of `^tken_[A-Za-z0-9_-]{43}$`, "the one response that reveals a token's secret; only its hash is stored"), with `Cache-Control: no-store`; `revokeEnrollmentToken` (`DELETE /enrollment-tokens/{tokenId}`) answers 204, again for a revoked or expired token, 404, or 422 `token-used` or `token-rotated` with `details.replacementTokenId`; rotate answers 404 or 422 `token-not-pending`. No ETag, revision, `Location`, or idempotency key exists on any of the four: a retry of issue mints a second token, and a retry of rotate after a lost 201 answers 422 with the new secret unrecoverable. 33a gives the `WorkersGateway` port, the typed fetch, `ReadResult` and `failureOf`, the wire guards and the scalar checks `isIdentifier`, `isDisplayText`, and `isUtcInstant`, `expiringCall`, the installation-owner gate, `LoadError`, `LoadingStatus`, `Breakable`, and `formatInstant`; 31b gives the CSRF getter of the session gateway and the single resend under the same `signedInGeneration`.

**Goals.** The enrollment tokens section of the workers screen: list, issue, revoke, and rotate over the extended port, the one-time reveal of the secret with its redaction tests, honest answers, and the link of the workers' empty state.

**Non-goals.** Account pools (33c), the 12d B backend, any OpenAPI or control-plane change (the CA fingerprint in the browser document and idempotency keys are follow-ups), a countdown or relative expiry, a "Load more" pager, a generic command-retry abstraction lifted from the composer, and any automatic retry of issue or rotate beyond the single CSRF resend.

## Decisions

1. **The port grows, the board does not (33a Q8 (b)).** `workers/workers.ts` adds the document's `Platform`, `Architecture`, `HostConstraints`, `EnrollmentToken`, and `IssuedEnrollmentToken`, an `EnrollmentTokenList` (`tokens` of every page and `truncated`), and the command results: `CommandRefused` (`csrf-rejected`, `invalid`, or a `ReadFailed`), `IssueResult` (`issued` with the `IssuedEnrollmentToken` or a refusal), `RevokeResult` (`revoked`, `token-used`, `token-rotated` with `replacementTokenId`, or a refusal), and `RotateResult` (an `IssueResult` or `token-not-pending`). `WorkersGateway` has the four methods beside `listWorkers`; `useWorkersGateway` wraps each with `expiringCall`, so a 401 `unauthenticated` of any of them ends the session in the shell and a gateway that throws reads as `unavailable`. `createWorkersGateway` takes a `csrfToken` getter as the board's does, default none, and `main.tsx` passes `session.csrfToken`; the typed client sends the header only with a request other than GET, so the two lists still carry none (the 33a test of that is unchanged).
2. **The paged read moves to `api/read-pages.ts`.** `readPages`, `MAX_PAGES`, and `Page` leave the board adapter for `api/read-pages.ts`; the board adapter imports `readPages` and re-exports `MAX_PAGES` and `MAX_PROJECT_PAGES`, so `control-plane-board.test.ts` and `agent-session-reads.test.ts` are unchanged and pass, and the board's lists behave as before: a cursor already followed loops and the page limit stops endless new ones, both reading as unreachable. The moved function is split in two so that the tokens can report the limit honestly (Q9 addition): `readPagesUpTo` follows the pages and answers `{ value, truncated }`, `truncated` when a new cursor would be the page beyond `MAX_PAGES` (the same count of requests the board's tests pin, 100), and `readPages` wraps it and reads a truncated list as `unavailable`, which is the board's old rule verbatim. The tokens adapter calls `readPagesUpTo` and shows the tokens read with the note "Only the first 100 pages of pending tokens are shown; the list is cut short." (`TRUNCATED_TEXT`, which takes the number from `MAX_PAGES`). Pending tokens live 15 minutes, so the limit is not a state an installation reaches; the note exists so the screen never claims a whole list it did not read.
3. **The bodies are checked and rebuilt (33a rule).** `workers/tokens-wire.ts` reads `EnrollmentToken` (`tokenId` an `Identifier`, `issuedAt` and `expiresAt` an `Instant`, `constraints` a record whose optional `platform`, `architecture`, and `hostname` are the two enums and `DisplayText`), `EnrollmentTokenPage` (at most 100 tokens, `next` an `Identifier` when present), and `IssuedEnrollmentToken` (the token and a `secret` of the documented pattern), copying only the documented fields into new objects, so a field planted outside the contract reaches no state and the list body, which "never" carries a secret, cannot carry one onto the page. `isPlatform` and `isArchitecture` join the exported scalar checks of `workers-wire.ts`. A 201 whose secret is not of the shape reveals nothing and reads as `unavailable`, so the page never shows a partial or foreign secret. The adapter maps the answers by the documented statuses and reasons, as 31b's `refusalOf` does: 400 `invalid`; 403 `csrf-token-invalid` as `csrf-rejected`, `role-required` with the role, any other 403 as `unavailable`; 404 as `not-found` for revoke and rotate, which document it, and as `unavailable` for issue and the list, which do not; 422 by its documented reason for the command that documents it (`token-used`, `token-rotated` with a `replacementTokenId` that is an `Identifier`, `token-not-pending`) and `unavailable` otherwise; a 400 of the list, which only a cursor the control plane itself named could cause, and every other status as `unavailable`; `Cache-Control: no-store` is the browser's concern and is not checked. `failureDetail` of `api/read-result.ts` reads one named detail of a `Failure`, which `failureReason` now uses.
4. **The list is a snapshot (Q8 (a), Q9 (a), Q10 (a)).** `useSnapshot` holds 33a's `loading | failed | shown(view, refreshing)` rule for any read, and `useWorkers` and `useEnrollmentTokens` are its two uses, so the tokens list keeps the shown list until the answer of a refresh, a failed refresh replaces it with `LoadError` and a Retry, and the cleanup drops an overtaken answer. The section shows the tokens as cards in the responsive grid of the workers, each with its identifier as the heading (monospace, breakable), "Issued <time>, expires <time>" as absolute `<time>` elements without a clock or countdown, and the constraints in words ("Any platform, any architecture, any hostname", "macOS, arm64, hostname mac-1.example"), a "Refresh tokens" button (named so beside the workers' Refresh), "Refreshing the tokens…", "No pending token.", and the truncation note. The commands read the list again through the snapshot's `refresh` after every answer that changed it or may have: a 201, a 204, and the 404 and 422 reasons, which mean the token left the pending list; a CSRF refusal, a denial, a 400, and no answer leave the list as shown, since nothing took effect or nothing is known, and the no-answer status offers the refresh instead.
5. **One command at a time, with the composer's CSRF resend (Q11 (a), Q12 (a)).** `workers/tokens-model.ts` holds the rules without React: `checkConstraints` turns the form into `HostConstraints` with only the chosen fields, the hostname trimmed and refused before it is sent when it is not `DisplayText`; `outcomeOf(command, result, tokenRefreshed)` maps each result to a settled answer (`issued`, `revoked`, `token-rotated`, `refused` with its reason, `denied` with the role, `failed` with `unavailable` or `unauthenticated`) or, for the first `csrf-rejected`, `retry-with-new-token`; `changesList`, `retryable`, and `suggestsRefresh` say what follows; `statusTextOf` gives the words. `useTokenCommands` runs one command at a time: an `inFlight` ref ignores a second `run` in the same tick, every Issue, Retry, Rotate, and Revoke button is a `CommandButton` that, while a command runs, says `aria-disabled` and `data-disabled` (Mantine's documented disabled look) and ignores clicks while staying focusable, so the focus is not lost when the answer comes, and the form's submit ignores a submit while busy; a `LoadingStatus` names the command ("Issuing a token…", "Revoking the token…", "Rotating the token…"). After a `csrf-rejected` the hook reads the session once through `refresh` and sends the command again only when the read answered, the same `signedInGeneration` still holds, and the section is still mounted; a session read without an answer, another operator, or no session settle as `token-not-renewed` or `session-changed` with nothing sent again, and a second refusal is final. This is safe for issue and rotate, which have no idempotency key, only because a `csrf-token-invalid` 403 "took no effect" by contract, so one resend is still at most one mint; no other failure of issue or rotate is retried, and the no-answer status says "Not issued, or issued but not shown" (or the rotate wording naming the token) with a "Refresh the list" button, while a revoke without an answer offers a Retry, since a repeat answers 204. The idempotency key for issue and rotate is a follow-up for the contract.
6. **The reveal (Q2 (a), Q3 (a), Q4 (b), Q5 (a)).** A 201 settles as `issued` and the hook puts `{ issued, replaced? }` in a `useState` of the section, the only holder of the secret: no context, store, storage, URL, turn-record store, module-level value, or closure outside React, and `console` is never called. The section renders the `RevealDialog`, a Mantine `Modal`, only while that state holds a secret, with `opened` always true, so the secret leaves the DOM the moment the state clears, without an exit transition; `closeOnEscape` and `closeOnClickOutside` are off and the dialog has no close button, only "Close". The dialog is titled "Enrollment token issued" or, after a rotation, "Token rotated" with "Token <old> stops working now." first; it shows the token's identifier, "valid for N minutes, until <time>" where N comes from `expiresAt - issuedAt` and never from a number of the SPA's, the constraints, the sentence that the secret is shown only now and Taskelith keeps only its hash, the secret in a read-only `TextInput` labelled "Enrollment token secret", a Copy button that writes the secret alone to the clipboard through `useClipboard({ timeout: 2000 })` and reads "Copied" for two seconds with "Copied to the clipboard." in a polite live region, "Could not copy; select the text and copy it." when the clipboard refuses or the browser has none, and the prose that the worker reads the secret from `TASKELITH_ENROLLMENT_TOKEN`, the control plane's address from `TASKELITH_CONTROL_PLANE_URL`, and the installation CA's fingerprint from `TASKELITH_INSTALLATION_CA_SHA256`, which the installation's CLI enrollment bundle prints; no command line is shown, since none exists and the SPA documents no CLI syntax. Close asks "Close without copying? The secret cannot be shown again." with Keep open and Close anyway unless a copy succeeded. The state clears on Close and Close anyway, is replaced by a rotation's 201, and is gone with the section on a route change, on the session's end, and on a sign-out, since the shell unmounts the page; an answer that settles after the section unmounted writes nothing (a `mounted` ref). Each path has its own test, each asserting the DOM, both storages, and the console. After the close the focus moves to the status region, which names what happened.
7. **The confirmations and the status region (Q6 (a), Q7 (a)).** Revoke (red, the light variant) and Rotate open a `Modal` titled "Revoke token" or "Rotate token" with "Revoke token <id>? A worker can no longer enroll with it. This cannot be undone." or "Rotate token <id>? The token stops working now, and a new one with the same constraints and a fresh expiry is shown once." and the buttons Revoke (red) or Rotate and Cancel; the dialog stays mounted with `opened` toggled and keeps the last question for its closing transition, so Mantine returns the focus to the row's button on Cancel. One `Alert role="status"` above the list, focusable and focused once an answer came and the reveal, if any, closed, shows the answer: "Token issued" or "Token rotated" naming the identifiers, "Token revoked", "Token already redeemed" ("… it is no longer pending. To stop that worker, revoke it with the CLI.", no command name), "Token already replaced" ("Token <id> was replaced by token <replacement>, which is still pending." with a "Show it" in-page anchor to the replacement's card, `id="enrollment-token-<id>"`, when the refreshed list holds it), "Token not pending", "Token not found", "Not <effect>" for a 400, a final CSRF refusal, a session read without an answer, or a changed sign-in, "No access to <verb> tokens" with the role a 403 names, and the no-answer texts of decision 5. The red light variant is a new drawn pair: Mantine's light scheme draws it as `red.9` on `red.0`, hovered on `red.2`, 3.8:1 by default, so the theme darkens `red.9` to `#b02020` (4.7:1 hovered, 5.6:1 at rest), which also darkens the light scheme's error color, the red alert title, and the failed self-check text, each of which the contrast matrix keeps above 4.5:1; the matrix gains "danger light" and "hovered danger light".
8. **The form (Q1 (a)).** An "Enroll a worker" button (`aria-expanded`) opens the form in place, rendered only while open, as a `Card` form named "Enroll a worker" with two ordered steps (issue a token here; start the worker with the secret in `TASKELITH_ENROLLMENT_TOKEN`), a `NativeSelect` for the platform (Any, Linux, macOS) and the architecture (Any, x64, arm64), a `TextInput` for the hostname ("Leave empty to accept any hostname."), and "Issue token". The POC's command line is replaced by the environment variable (departure below).
9. **The empty state's link and the gate.** The workers' "No worker is enrolled. Enroll one with an enrollment token." links "enrollment token" to `#enrollment-tokens`, the section's `id`; the "No worker" scenario of "Web SPA workers screen" is modified for it, and the 33a page test of the empty state asserts the link instead of its absence. `WorkersPage` renders the section after the workers for an owner and the one `RoleNotice` for anyone else, so a session without the role sends neither read. The section and the workers keep their own hook and states: a token command never blanks the workers grid and a failed workers read never hides the tokens.

## Departures

- **The POC's command line.** `docs/ui-poc/src/screens/Workers.tsx` shows `taskelith worker enroll --server … --token …`, which does not exist as a CLI subcommand; the worker reads `TASKELITH_ENROLLMENT_TOKEN` from its environment (`apps/worker/src/settings.ts`). The form's steps and the reveal name the variable and no command (Q2 addition).
- **The POC's four steps.** The POC's "Enroll a worker" section describes the install, the self-check, and the admission in four steps; the form keeps two, since the self-check results already show on the worker's card above (33a) and the SPA does not document the install.
- **The POC has no token list, revoke, rotate, or reveal.** They come from the contract and the brief.
- **`readPages` moved and split.** The decisions call the lift a pure move; `readPages` keeps its behaviour and the board's tests are unchanged, but its body now lives in `readPagesUpTo`, which the tokens use for the honest truncation note (Q9 addition). Recorded here so the reviewer can judge the split against the "pure move" wording.

## Follow-ups

- **Idempotency keys on `issueEnrollmentToken` and `rotateEnrollmentToken`** (OpenAPI and control-plane change): without one, a lost 201 leaves a pending token whose secret nobody has, which the operator revokes or rotates from the list (Q12 addition).
- **The installation CA fingerprint in the browser document** (OpenAPI change): `readInstallationCa` is in the execution document only, so the reveal can only say where the fingerprint comes from (Q2 addition).
- **The account pools (33c)** add their section below the tokens and extend `WorkersGateway`.

## Proof of concept

The section follows `docs/ui-poc/src/screens/Workers.tsx` (`WorkersScreen`): an "Enroll worker" button that expands an "Enroll a worker" section in place, and the responsive grid of the workers. Departures, each with its requirement or decision: the command line (above, Q2); the four steps (above); the list, the commands, the confirmations, and the reveal, which the POC lacks (the brief); the whole page gated, not only the actions (33a Q2 (a)).

## Risks and trade-offs

- A CSRF resend of issue or rotate relies on the contract's "nothing took effect" for a `csrf-token-invalid` 403; a control plane that minted a token and then answered 403 would mint twice. The contract and `apps/control-plane/src/api/routes.ts` check the CSRF token before the body, so the SPA follows the document.
- A lost 201 leaves a pending token whose secret is unrecoverable and a status that says so; the follow-up for the contract is the idempotency key.
- The list is read again after every answer that changed it, one read per command; the server is the authority on "pending", so no local merge is attempted.
- The reveal's focus goes to the status region after the close, and the confirmation's focus returns to the row's button on Cancel; after a confirmed revoke the row leaves with the refreshed list, so the focus moves to the status region as well.
- `useClipboard` keeps "Copied" for two seconds through a timer of Mantine's; the tests assert the label and the announcement and not the reset.
- The theme's `red.9` darkens the light scheme's error color and the red alert title as well as the new button; every pair stays above 4.5:1 and no screen depends on the exact shade.

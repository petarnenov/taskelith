## Why

G2 item 13 added `rotate-worker`, which issues a new worker credential generation, and item 20a stores the worker's credentials under `worker/credentials/`. No plan item owned delivering a rotated generation to the worker. On 2026-10-25, while the owner was away, the owner's delegated decision-maker (a Claude Fable 5.1 consul, under the owner's delegation of that evening) decided to defer delivery to G3 item 26 and to record the G2 limitation.

## What Changes

- `docs/contracts/installation-and-enrollment.md` section 6: in G2, rotating a worker means enrolling it again; delivery belongs to G3 item 26. The "Not yet" note points there.
- `docs/g2-plan.md`: a "Recorded limitations" section that item 27 lists among the closure's deferrals.
- `docs/g3-plan.md` item 26: its scope adds delivery and atomic adoption of a rotated generation.

# Design: build-task-detail

## Context

G3 item 29 (`docs/g3-plan.md`, row 29), sub-change 29b. Its base is 29a `build-board-view` ([archive](/openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-03-build-board-view/design.md)): the `BoardGateway` port, the board feed, the reduced-motion loading line, and the reserved route `/projects/:projectId/cards/:runId`. The server side is `readTaskDetail` of 15a ([archive](/openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-03-implement-board-and-inbox-views/design.md)), product ports section 3.7: one snapshot with `asOf`, `position`, the card, the stages, `traceability` (`[]` until item 25), the run binding, and the tasks in admission order.

**Goals.** The task detail of product UI "Task detail" on its own route as a Mantine Drawer, its states, its live updates through the 29a feed, and the three 29a follow-ups.

**Non-goals.** Answering inbox items (item 32), new task (item 30), the agent session screen (item 31), any OpenAPI or control-plane change, and the audited reconciliation of an uncertain process.

## Decisions

1. **The port grows one read.** `BoardGateway` gains `readTaskDetail(projectId, runId)` with the 29a `ReadResult`; `control-plane-board.ts` sends `GET /projects/{projectId}/cards/{runId}` through the typed fetch, with the 29a mapping of 401, 403, 404, and other statuses. The view types are the generated OpenAPI types.
2. **Body checked for what the Drawer shows.** `task-detail-wire.ts` checks every field the Drawer renders, reusing the 29a checks of the card, session activity, and obstacle: stages with origin, state, column, and gates (state, required evidence, optional block reason); trace links with their identifier lists and freshness; all eight run binding fields; tasks with identifiers, ownership generation of at least 1, admission, delegation depth 1 to 3, session, obstacle, and a task end whose outcome kind, end time, exit status, or operator evidence is readable. A body that fails, or the detail of another card than the one asked for, reads as `unavailable`. The label test keeps the new records (`EVIDENCE_KIND_LABELS`, `FRESHNESS_LABELS`, `ADMISSION_STATE_LABELS`, `OUTCOME_LABELS`) equal to their OpenAPI enums or `oneOf` constants.
3. **A child route of the board.** `routes.tsx` makes `BoardPage` a pathless layout route with an empty index and `TASK_DETAIL.path` as children, so following a card link keeps the board mounted, its feed running, and nothing read again. `BoardPage` now calls `useLiveBoard` itself, for the chosen project or none, and hands its `Outlet` the project, the live board with its retry, and a function that focuses the board's heading; `useLiveBoard` never returns the state of another project than the one asked for, which the 29a `key` remount used to ensure. `TaskDetailRoute` opens `TaskDetailDrawer`, keyed by project and run. A card link marks its history entry with the page load (`board-entry.ts`), so closing a detail it opened goes back one entry and Back does not open it again; a detail opened from a direct link or a reload, whose entry has no such mark, closes by replacing its address with the board, so closing never leaves the app (pass 2 finding 5, coordinator decision, 2026-11-03). Because the Drawer leaves with its route, Mantine's focus return, which needs a Drawer that closes while mounted, never runs: `TaskDetailRoute` keeps the element that had the focus when it opened and, when it leaves by Escape, its close button, or the browser's back, focuses that element again, the card link, or else the board's heading, for a detail opened from its address (pass 1 finding 3). `TASK_DETAIL` leaves `PLANNED_SCREENS`.
4. **The address's project.** `BoardPage` selects the address's `projectId` in `ProjectsProvider` whenever it changes, which also keeps it for the next visit. The selection runs on mount, before `listProjects` answers, so no other project's board is read first. An unlisted project falls back to the first, as in 29a, and the Drawer then shows the `readTaskDetail` failure. The Drawer is modal, so the header's picker behind its overlay is out of reach while it is open; the Drawer therefore shows the same Mantine `ProjectPicker` at its top (pass 1 finding 4). Choosing another project there navigates to the board, so the Drawer never shows over a board of another project; choosing one in the header's picker on any other page keeps that page (coordinator decision, 2026-11-03). The picker appears twice because the modal Drawer keeps its focus trap and Escape rather than letting the header's picker through its overlay; both pickers read and write the one selection of `ProjectsProvider`, so they cannot disagree (coordinator question Q3 of pass 2).
5. **Content.** `TaskDetailView` (product ports section 3.7, product UI "Task detail") shows: the card's work type, framework, run, column, step (native identifier or "platform-managed"), stage state, session indicator, and merge gate state; "Why it does not move" with the card's wait, placement obstacle, each gate with a block reason, and a task end, left out when nothing holds the card; "Stages and gates", an ordered list in graph order of bordered stages, each with its step, column, and state, and its gates, each gate its identifier beside a dot badge naming its state, `stale` included, and the evidence it needs; "Traceability", a list of bordered links, each with its requirement, work items, attempts, and a freshness badge, or the sentence that the control plane records none for the run yet; "Run binding", a row-header table of the eight fields; and "Tasks in admission order" (`TaskList`), an ordered list with each task's run, ownership generation, delegation, admission, session indicator, obstacle, and task end with its attempt outcome and end time. Every identifier, the digest, and display text shown break every 16 characters of a run without a space through `<wbr>` (`Breakable`), and only the run binding stays a two-column table, so nothing forces sideways scrolling at phone width ("Design rules") and a copy stays unchanged; four-column tables could not fit 128-character identifiers there (pass 1 finding 6). The Drawer's title is plain text in Mantine's own `h2` (pass 1 finding 7). The lists are Mantine `Stack` elements rendered as `ol` or `ul` (`ItemList`) whose items are `Paper` or `Text` elements rendered as `li`, because Mantine's `List.Item` wraps its content in a `span`, which may hold no block (pass 2 finding 3); each section is named by its `h3` through `aria-labelledby`, not by a second label (pass 2 finding 4). `stepOf` lives in `board-labels.ts` and `ObstacleText` in its own module, which the card and the detail both use (pass 2 finding 8).
6. **States.** The Drawer shows `LoadingStatus` "Loading the task detail…" while the first read runs; a failure with a retry (`LoadError`, now shared with the board) and no task data, naming the role a 403 needs; and, while the board's liveness is `lost`, a notice with the detail's `asOf`. A failed board of the detail's project, such as a 403 on the re-read after `stream-ended` or no board after a closed stream, drops the detail in the same way, with the board's role and a retry of the board and the detail, because the board read is the one that shows the caller's new roles (wire contracts section 10; pass 1 finding 1). Its title is the card's title once read, "Task detail" before. A 401 `unauthenticated` reaches the session through the 29a `useBoardServices` wrapper, which now wraps `readTaskDetail` too.
7. **Live updates.** `startTaskDetailFeed` (`task-detail-feed.ts`, plain TypeScript) reads the detail and offers `cardChanged(position)` and `boardReread()`. `cardChanged` reads again only when the position is above the detail's, compared as `BigInt`; one read runs at a time, and the latest change that arrives during a read causes one read after it if the answer is older. `boardReread` reads again whatever the positions, because a resync `cursor-unknown` after a restore can set the sequence back (wire contracts section 10), and drops the changes held from before; during a read it causes one read after it. A failed read drops the detail, so later changes read nothing until a retry starts a new feed; stopping it drops a late answer. The board feed numbers its snapshots (`ShownBoard.snapshot`); `useTaskDetail` calls `boardReread` for every new one, with or without the card, and otherwise `cardChanged` with the board's position whenever the board view holds a new object for the card, which the 29a feed makes on every `card-changed` of it; a change of another card keeps the object and reads nothing (pass 1 finding 2). The detail's first read starts with the Drawer, independently of the board; the board's first snapshot then only compares positions. Without a board of its project, as for an unlisted project, it is one snapshot. Each new detail feed, on opening or on a retry, forgets the snapshot and the card it saw, because a restarted board feed numbers its snapshots from 1 again (pass 2 finding 1).
8. **Session check loader.** 29a's reduced-motion loading line of `BoardPage` moves to `shell/LoadingStatus.tsx` and also renders the shell's "Checking your session…", which now gains the role `status`; under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` (`useReducedMotion`) no `Loader` renders.
9. **Card link.** The card's title is a Mantine `Anchor` with the router's `Link` to its detail; the card stays an `article` named by it. The contrast test adds the anchor color on a card in both schemes; every other pair the Drawer draws (text and dimmed text on the body, dot badges, the indigo and red alerts) is already tested.
10. **Route-safe identifiers.** The SPA's server answers 404 for a last path segment with a dot, which it takes for a file, and for a segment whose decoding holds a separator (28c, `web-app.ts`), while identifiers are any 1 to 128 printable ASCII characters. `taskDetailPath` therefore writes each character other than a letter, a digit, `-`, or `_` as `~` and its two hexadecimal digits, so `run.1` becomes `run~2E1`, and `identifierOf` reads it back before any read; the result needs no percent-encoding (pass 1 finding 5). `identifierOf` reads only the uppercase form that `taskDetailPath` writes, so one task has one address; the control plane decodes only percent-encoding (`web-app.ts`, `buildPath`) and knows no `~` form (pass 2 finding 7, declined by the coordinator's rule).

## Agent sessions

The item brief lists the card's agent sessions from `listAgentSessions` in the task detail; the 29b brief's scope list does not. Each `TaskView` carries its own `SessionActivity`, so the Drawer shows every task's session indicator from `readTaskDetail` alone, and `listAgentSessions`, which adds harness names, session identifiers, and limits but no activity state, is left to the agent session screen (item 31). The coordinator decided so on 2026-11-03: the card's agent sessions from `listAgentSessions` are a follow-up of item 31, and the Drawer keeps the session indicator from `readTaskDetail`.

## Proof of concept

The Drawer follows `RunDrawer` and `RunDetail` of `docs/ui-poc/src/screens/BoardDrawers.tsx` (right `Drawer` of size `xl`, sections with `h3`-level titles, the stage table, admission, traceability, and run binding sections). Departures, each required by a requirement or decision ("Reference proof of concept"):

- **Address, not state.** POC: the Drawer opens from local state. It opens from its route (decision Q2).
- **No demonstration flows.** POC: `ReconcileAction`, `RuntimeApproval`, the agent session log, and the "Change framework…" button act on fixtures. No mock data (item 28 decision), no operation of this change acts, and the session screen is item 31.
- **Reasons as a list.** POC: "Why it is not moving" is an orange Alert of one sentence. The answer has several sources (wait, obstacle, gate reasons, task end), listed as text, because Mantine's orange light alert title is 3.62:1 in the light scheme (29a).
- **No "later" badges.** POC: traceability names G3 items 15 and 17 as later. The answer is shown, with an honest empty state while `traceability` is `[]`.
- **Tasks.** POC: one admission line per run. The detail lists every task in admission order, delegated ones included.
- **Stacked stages and links.** POC: the stages are a table. Stages and trace links are bordered lists, because a four-column table of 128-character identifiers cannot fit at phone width ("Design rules").
- **Picker in the Drawer.** POC: no picker. The modal Drawer shows the project picker, which the overlay hides in the header.

The coordinator accepted the first five departures and the trade-offs of the design that pass 1 reviewed on 2026-11-03, beside the decisions on agent sessions, the picker, and the removed requirement; the last two departures follow pass 1 findings 6 and 4, and pass 2 reviews them.

## Risks and trade-offs

- The detail is read again on every board re-read (resync, `stream-ended`, a closed stream), whatever its position, because a new snapshot can lie below the detail after a restore; the reads are serial and bounded by the board's own re-read limits.
- When a detail is opened from its address, its read and the board's run concurrently, and the board's first snapshot only compares positions: when the board answers above the detail's position, because a card of the project changed between the two reads, the detail is read once more, after its first read if that still runs; a board at or below the detail's position reads nothing more. The read is not skipped when the board's card equals the detail's, because a change of the detail alone, such as a stage, a gate, or a task, can leave the card unchanged (pass 2 finding 2).
- Wire contracts sections 8 and 10 require a `card-changed` event after any change of the card or its task detail, and 15a emits it. A control plane that omitted one would violate that contract; the detail would then show the change only on the next board re-read or a reopen.
- Choosing a project from the header's picker on any other page keeps that page; only a task-detail address leads back to the board (coordinator decision, 2026-11-03, and the spec scenario "Another project on another page").

## Why

G3 item 2 (`review-g3-provider-terms`, merged in #214) recorded what the Anthropic, OpenAI, and GitHub terms say about Taskelith's use, and left D15 to the owner. On 2026-10-25 the owner said, in Bulgarian and paraphrased here, that the subscription pool can be very large: subscriptions of many people who have delegated the pool's management, across different harnesses. Shown the terms that appear to prohibit pooled or intermediated subscription use, the owner chose D15 option (a), accepting the remaining risk.

## What Changes

- `docs/g3-plan.md`: D15 is recorded as decided, with the owner's intent, the accepted risk, and the condition that the product warns a subscriber before delegating (items 12 and 33). D15 no longer blocks items 35 and 36.
- `docs/qualification/claude-code.md`, `codex.md`, `github.md`: "D15 is pending" becomes the decision, with a link to the plan.

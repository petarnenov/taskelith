## Why

The owner requested the next step after reading the project documentation. Bootstrap publication and automated releases are verified, but G0 still lacks a versioned policy defining lifecycle authority and evidence. Establish that policy before implementing G1 contracts.

## What Changes

- Document bootstrap roles, lifecycle transitions, evidence freshness, and integration authority.
- Record the selected framework and its runtime-independent bootstrap use in an architecture decision.
- Publish a G0 status checklist that distinguishes completed work from unresolved decisions.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `bootstrap-governance`: Repository-based lifecycle policy and foundation readiness tracking.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Repository documentation only; no runtime, CLI, dependency, deployment, or publication changes. The owner's continuation request authorizes this bounded G0 work item, not the entire roadmap.

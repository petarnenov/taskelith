## Decisions

1. **The port grows three reads.** `BoardGateway` gains `listAgentSessions(projectId, runId)`, every page as `listProjects` reads its pages (the paging loop is now shared as `readPages`, with the same cursor loop and page limit), `readAgentSession(projectId, sessionId)`, and `listAgentSessionTurns(projectId, sessionId, after)`, one page, with the 29a mapping of 401, 403, 404, and other statuses. A session of another card, a session other than the one asked for, and a turn at or below the cursor are wrong answers and read as `unavailable`. `BoardServices` routes the three reads into the session's expiry as the board reads.
2. **Bodies checked for what the screen shows.** `agent-session-wire.ts` checks the session (identifiers, harness, cursors, output window, running generation and ownership generation of at least 1, limit, active attempt, handoff with its request keys), every cursor, position, and generation a safe integer, as the OpenAPI schemas cap them at 9007199254740991 (pass 1 finding 1), each turn (identifier, position of at least 1, principal or host author with its delegation request key, text, a state of `TurnState`, the generation of a non-queued turn, the outcome of an ended one, and the failure of a failed one), and the stamps of the views and events. `session-labels.ts` names the turn states, outcomes, and harness failures, and a test keeps the records equal to the OpenAPI enums. Both live beside `board-wire.ts` and `board-labels.ts` under `board/`, as does `useCardSessions.ts`, which the task detail Drawer uses, so `board/` imports nothing from `agent-session/`, which builds on it (pass 2 finding 6).
3. **One stream, two event sets.** `openEvents` also listens to `agent-session-changed` and `turn-changed`, delivering them as `SessionChange` and `TurnChange` of the `StreamChange` union, and an unreadable one reads as `resync` as before. `output-appended`, `inbox-item-changed`, and `native-request-changed` are not listened to: the browser's `EventSource` keeps `Last-Event-ID` from every frame it receives, listened to or not, and a feed opens a new stream only after a fresh read, so no cursor needs them.
4. **The reconnect core is shared.** `live-feed.ts` holds the 29a state machine over a `LiveView<View>` (`read`, `positionOf`, `apply`) and an `openEvents(after, listener)`: snapshot, stream after its position, changes during a read applied over its snapshot up to 1,000, resync with its growing delay, read on `stream-ended`, read then reopen 10 s after a closed stream. `startBoardFeed` is now a thin wrapper that keeps its signature and exports, and `board-feed.test.ts` runs unmodified. The wrapper drops the stream's session events before they reach the core (`boardEventsOnly`), so they count neither as a delivered change for the resync delay nor toward the during-read cap, and the board's reconnect behaviour is the one its unmodified tests observe (pass 1 finding 8; `board-feed-session-events.test.ts` checks both); `applyChange` of the board still leaves the view as it is for one. The core gains `amend`, for a read outside the stream.
5. **Session view.** `SessionTimelineView` holds the session, the turns in position order, `before` (the turns before the earliest window read, the cursor of the next earlier read), the run's card as the stream last showed it, the lowest position and earliest `asOf` of its reads, and the `freshness` of each record: the position of the read or event that supplied the session and each turn by `turnId` (wire contracts section 10; pass 1 finding 7). `applySessionChange` applies a `turn-changed` or `agent-session-changed` of the session above the view's position and above the freshness of the record it replaces, whole records replacing by `turnId` or the session, keeps the card of a `card-changed` of the session's run, and returns the same view for any other event, so nothing is shown again for it; so a turn read at position 50 keeps its state under a replayed event at 45 while the session read at 40 takes it, in either direction, also when the stream is interrupted during the replay. The view's `knownAt` keeps the later of the snapshot's time and an event's, so a delayed event never moves it back, while a new snapshot sets its own (finding 5). A changed turn before the window is shown in its position order; the window's cursor does not move for it.
6. **Last window first (Q5).** The feed reads the session, then the turns after `max(0, lastTurnPosition - 100)`, following `next` for turns the session gained between the two reads, bounded by 100 pages. "Load earlier" reads the pages after `max(0, before - 100)`, following a short page's `next` cursor until it reaches `before`, since the contract promises at most 100 turns per page (finding 2), with the same loop guards; a turn the page carries replaces a known one only when the page was read above the read or event that supplied it (finding 7), and `before` moves down to the window's cursor. One earlier read runs at a time, a failed one shows why, with the role a 403 names, and a retry (`EarlierRead` carries the `ReadFailed`; finding 3), and one that a new snapshot overtook is dropped, because the new snapshot shows the last window again.
7. **Activity (Q1).** `readTaskDetail(projectId, session.runId)` answers `tasks[].session` per task; the page joins by `session.taskId` and renders the 29a `SessionIndicator`. `useTaskDetail` now takes a `LiveCard` (the card as a live view last showed it, the view's position, and its snapshot count) instead of a `ShownBoard`, so the task detail feed re-reads on a `card-changed` of the run above the detail's position and on each new session snapshot exactly as under the board; `liveCardOf` makes the `LiveCard` of the Drawer, whose tests run unmodified. A detail without the session's task says so, its identifiers through `Breakable` (finding 6), and shows no indicator.
8. **Address and links (Q2, Q3).** `/app/sessions/:projectId/:sessionId` with `sessionPath` and `identifierOf` of the task detail address. `/app/sessions` is no longer a planned page: it says that a session opens from its task and leads to the board, because `listAgentSessions` is per card. The task detail lists the sessions with each detail shown, keeps the last list while a new one is read, and links each task to its sessions by harness and identifier; a failed list shows why, by failure kind, with the role a 403 names, and a retry, beside the tasks (finding 4).
9. **Honest states.** Loading lines through `LoadingStatus` (no loader under reduced motion), failures through `LoadError` with the 29b texts adapted, "No turn yet.", the lost notice with the view's `knownAt`, and a 401 `unauthenticated` answer of any session read showing the sign-in page.
10. **Rendering.** Mantine `Timeline` for the turns, a dot `Badge` for the state, `Breakable` for identifiers and each line of a turn's text (split on newlines, since D17 allows no `white-space` override). The contrast test adds the anchor on the page body; dot badges, alerts, and dimmed text are already tested.

## Departures

- **Process state uncertain (Q7).** Product UI "Session activity indicator" says the card offers an audited reconciliation; as 29a and 29b, this change shows the state only (still, danger color, label), because the contract has no reconciliation operation and the action belongs to the inbox (items 15b and 32). Recorded as a departure from the scenario, accepted by the coordinator.
- **Resync resets the window.** A new snapshot after a resync, a stream end, or a closed stream shows the last window again, dropping earlier windows the operator loaded, which "load earlier" reads again; the alternative, re-reading from the loaded cursor, would make every resync cost as many pages as were loaded.

## Follow-ups

- **Output tail (Q4).** `readAgentSessionOutput` and `output-appended` with an honest notice when the cursor expired (`result: cursor-expired`, `earliestRetained`), as item 14's design asks; not in the plan row and deferred by the coordinator.
- **Composer (31b).** `submitTurn`, the CSRF token exposure, the idempotency key per composed message in `sessionStorage`, and the pending, failed, duplicate, conflict, refused, and session-stopped states.
- **Native requests** of a session, read-only, go with item 32.

## Proof of concept

The screen follows `docs/ui-poc/src/screens/Session.tsx`: the header with the session's facts, the `Timeline` of "Turn history" with one item per turn, and the working state through the indicator. Departures, each required by a requirement or decision ("Reference proof of concept"):

- **No composer and no stop button.** POC: a `TextInput` with a send `RoleButton` and "Stop session". The composer is 31b (Q9), and the contract has no stop operation; a placeholder would be dishonest.
- **No conversation bubbles, no live output, no tool or edit entries.** POC: entries of several kinds from fixtures and a "Live output" terminal. The contract's turns carry author, text, and state only, and the output tail is a follow-up (Q4); no mock data (item 28).
- **Turn states from the contract.** POC: "accepted, delivered at the next turn boundary" or "sending" per user entry. Each turn shows its `TurnState` label, outcome, or failure, and its generation.
- **Indicator, not badge.** POC: `SessionBadge` and `LiveStatus`. The SPA's `SessionIndicator` (29a) and the lost notice (29a) replace them.
- **Earlier turns on demand.** POC: every turn. The last window first (Q5).
- **No breadcrumbs.** POC: Board / card / Session. The run link in the summary leads to the task detail, and the shell has no breadcrumb component yet.

## Risks and trade-offs

- A turn whose `turn-changed` arrives before its window is loaded shows above the window; the operator sees a gap in the positions until "load earlier" fills it. Honest, and rare: turns before the last 100 seldom change.
- The activity comes from a second read with its own position; a `card-changed` of the run below the detail's position is skipped, and the next above re-reads it, as under the board.
- Session events reach the board's `openEvents` listener, which drops them before the core (decision 4), so a busy session changes neither the board's resync delay nor its during-read cap; the cost is one more check per event on the board.
- The `freshness` map holds one position per known turn, at most 100 per window loaded; it is replaced with each new snapshot.

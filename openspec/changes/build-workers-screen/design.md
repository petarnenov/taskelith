# Design: build-workers-screen

## Context

G3 item 33 (`docs/g3-plan.md`, row 33), sub-change 33a under the coordinator's split (`brief-33-plan.md`, Niki, 2026-11-04). Its base is `dev` `d0acd304` (#445) with change 1 of the item, `serve-workers-and-enrollment-tokens` ([archive](/openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-04-serve-workers-and-enrollment-tokens/design.md)): the control plane serves `listWorkers` (`GET /workers`, `control-plane.openapi.json` 0.5.0): the role `installation-owner` is checked before the body, every self-check detail and hostname is redacted again in the handler, and the answer is the whole `WorkersView` (`asOf`, `workers[]` of `WorkerAdmissionEntry` "every enrolled worker that is not revoked, in identifier order; never partial") or 503 without partial data; 401 and 403 (`AccessDenial` with `details.reason` `role-required` and `details.requiredRole`) are the other documented answers; no 404 and no query parameter. `WorkerAdmissionEntry` is `workerId`, `host` (`platform` `linux | darwin`, `architecture` `x64 | arm64`, `hostname` `DisplayText`), `enrolledAt`, `admission` (`awaiting-self-check | admitted | self-check-failed`), optional `reportedAt`, and `results`, up to 8 `SelfCheckResult` in check identifier order, a `oneOf` by `outcome`: `passed` with no details, `failed` with 1 to 8 `DisplayText` problems, `not-applicable` with exactly one `NotApplicableReason` (`platform-not-covered | no-launch-command`). The web shell of 28, the board of 29a and 29b, and the session screen of 31a and 31b give the typed fetch (`api/client.ts`), the `ReadResult` shape, `expiringGateway` of `BoardServices`, `LoadError` with `withRequiredRole`, `LoadingStatus`, `Breakable`, `formatInstant`, the planned-screen registry (`screens.ts`), and the contrast matrix.

**Goals.** The workers screen with the admission and self-check results of every enrolled worker, a `WorkersGateway` port of its own, the installation-owner gate, `asOf` with a Refresh, honest states, and the redaction test.

**Non-goals.** Enrollment tokens (33b), account slots (33c), any OpenAPI or control-plane change, per-worker reachability, capacity, and last contact (departure, see below), live updates (follow-up), revoked workers (not on the wire).

## Decisions

1. **A workers port of its own (Q8 (b)).** `workers/workers.ts` is the port: the view types of the generated OpenAPI document, `INSTALLATION_OWNER` typed by the document's `InstallationRole`, and `WorkersGateway.listWorkers(): Promise<ReadResult<WorkersView>>`. The read outcome types `ReadFailure`, `ReadFailed`, and `ReadResult` move from `board/board.ts` to `api/read-result.ts`, with `failureOf` (401 `unauthenticated` by reason, 403 with the role, 404, else unavailable), `denialOf`, `failureReason`, `requiredRoleOf`, and `reachable`, which the board adapter kept privately; `board.ts` and `board-wire.ts` re-export them, so every existing import, test included, is unchanged. `main.tsx` creates the gateway with `createWorkersGateway()` and `App` takes it as `workers`, held by a `WorkersServicesProvider` of its own next to `BoardServicesProvider`; 33b and 33c add their operations to this port.
2. **The adapter and the body check.** `workers/control-plane-workers.ts` sends `GET /workers` through `createApiClient` with a getter that answers no CSRF token, since the client sends the token only with a request other than GET and the port has none. `workers-wire.ts` checks every field the screen shows against the document's scalars and rebuilds the view from them alone: `asOf`, `enrolledAt`, and the optional `reportedAt` as `Instant` (the pattern `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm:ss.sssZ` and a date `Date.parse` accepts); `workerId` as `Identifier` (1 to 128 printable ASCII characters); the hostname and each problem as `DisplayText` (1 to 1,024 code points, no C0 or C1 control character, no DEL); one of the three admission states; a host of the two platforms and two architectures; and at most 8 results, each with a `checkId` of the document's pattern and the details its outcome fixes (none, 1 to 8 problems, or one of the two reasons). `isIdentifier`, `isDisplayText`, and `isUtcInstant` are exported for 33b and 33c; the board's looser `isText` and `isInstant` are unchanged, so its behaviour is too. The view holds no object of the body: the problems of a failed check are copied into a new array, so a property planted on the body's array, or a later change to it, never reaches the view (pass 1 findings 1 and 2). A field outside the contract never reaches the view, so a planted value in one reaches no state (decision 8). A body that fails any check, a 404, a 5xx, an undocumented status, an HTML page, or no answer reads as `unavailable`, the 29a rule; the 503 that the contract documents for a source the control plane cannot read is thereby "no partial data" on the screen too.
3. **The expiry wrapper is shared.** The `checked` and `settle` of `BoardServices.expiringGateway` move to `session/expiring-call.ts` as `expiringCall(expiryOfRequest)`, which the board's wrapper and `useWorkersGateway` of `WorkersServices.tsx` both apply: a 401 `unauthenticated` of `listWorkers` ends the session in the shell as the board's reads do, and a gateway that throws reads as `unavailable`.
4. **The gate (Q2 (a)).** `WorkersPage` reads the session view of `useSession`; with `installation-owner` among the operator's `installationRoles` it mounts `Workers`, whose hook reads; without it, it renders the heading and a gray `Alert role="status"` titled "Needs installation-owner" that says nothing was read, and mounts no reader, so no request is sent. The navigation lists the screen for every operator (product UI "Web SPA screen routes"). The session's roles are a snapshot of the last successful session read: `SessionProvider` reads the session again at the end it announced and on `refresh`, and an authenticated answer replaces the operator with its roles, so the gate mounts or unmounts the reader without a new sign-in, and the cleanup of the reader's effect drops the answer of a read that a revoke overtook (pass 1 finding 7; the page test covers a grant and a revoke through the expiry check). A 403 from the control plane shows the denied state with the role `withRequiredRole` names, so the control plane stays the authority between reads.
5. **One snapshot and Refresh (Q5 (a)).** `useWorkers` keeps `loading`, `failed` with the `ReadFailed`, or `shown` with the view and a `refreshing` flag. A load counter drives the effect; Refresh increments it. While a view is shown, the effect marks it `refreshing` instead of blanking it, and the page shows "Refreshing the workers…" beside the Refresh button with the list still visible; a `read` answer replaces the view and a failure replaces the whole list with `LoadError`, so no stale list is shown as current (the board's rule). The effect's cleanup drops the answer of a read that an unmount or a later refresh overtook. The screen stays within the brief's scope of a snapshot: no clock, no timer, no stream.
6. **Order and the states in words (Q3 (a), Q6 (a), Q7 (a)).** The workers are shown in the wire's order, which the contract fixes as identifier order; a failed worker is made visible by its state, not by its position. `workers-labels.ts` holds the words: the admission states ("Awaiting self-check", "Admitted", "Not admitted: self-check failed"), the platforms ("Linux", "macOS"), the outcomes, and the reasons ("platform not covered", "no launch command"); `failedFirst` orders a worker's results with the failed checks first and keeps the wire's check identifier order within each group. The cards sit in the POC's responsive `SimpleGrid` (`cols={{ base: 1, sm: 2, xl: 4 }}`) rendered as the `section` named "Workers", one column at phone width. Each worker is a Mantine `Card` rendered as an `article` labelled by its hostname heading, with the identifier in monospace, the host and enrolment line, the admission state, "Last report <time>" or "No report yet.", and, when results exist, a `List` named "Self-checks" whose items are the check identifier in monospace and the outcome in words; a failed check adds a nested `List` of its problems, a skipped one its reason after a colon. The item's text is a `Text component="div"`, since a paragraph may not hold a list (pass 1 finding 3), and the page test fails on any unexpected console output, React's HTML warnings included. No badge is drawn, so none can be interactive. A failed admission and a failed check draw the theme's error color (`--mantine-color-error`, red.9 light and red.5 dark, set by the theme's resolver of 31b) through the `c` prop; the contrast matrix gains "a failed self-check on a worker card" (the error color on the card surface, 5.46:1 light and 4.89:1 dark) beside the page pair 31b added. Every text that may run without a space, the hostname, the identifier, the check identifier, and each problem, goes through `Breakable`, so the card never scrolls sideways at phone width.
7. **Empty and failed views (Q4 (a)).** An empty `workers` shows the `asOf` line, Refresh, and a dimmed "No worker is enrolled. Enroll one with an enrollment token." without a link, since the token screen is 33b. A failed read shows `LoadError` with a title and text per failure kind and a Retry that reads again: "Your session ended" for a late 401, "The workers cannot be loaded" for an unreachable plane, "No access to the workers" with the role for a denial, and, for the `not-found` kind the shared type still carries, a text saying that the control plane knows no workers view, which the adapter never produces (decision 2) but a port implementation could. The loading line "Loading the workers…" and the refreshing line go through `LoadingStatus`, which draws no loader under reduced motion.
8. **Redaction (Q9 (a) and (b)).** The page holds the view in the hook's component state only, never in a context, a storage, or a log; the problems and hostnames are rendered as text, never parsed or linked. The test "shows the redacted texts as received and lets no secret shape reach the page, a storage, or the console" runs the real session, board, and workers gateways over a scripted control plane whose view carries a hostname and a problem redacted as the server sends them (`[redacted]`) and an entry field outside the contract holding an enrollment-token shape (`tken_` and 43 characters): the page shows the redacted texts, the HTML holds neither that shape nor the session's CSRF token, `sessionStorage` and `localStorage` have no key, and no console argument, an `Error`'s stack included, holds either. The wire and adapter tests check that the planted field is dropped before the view reaches any state.
9. **Routing and the registry.** `WORKERS` of `screens.ts` is exported, names this change, and leaves `PLANNED_SCREENS` through a `BUILT_SCREENS` list with `BOARD` and `SESSIONS`; `routes.tsx` routes `/workers` to `WorkersPage`. The title "Workers and account pools" stays (Q1 (a)), so `app.test.tsx` and `navigation.test.tsx` pass unchanged: their assertions are the heading of the login landing and the navbar choice, which the built screen keeps, and the planned-page loop of `navigation.test.tsx` iterates `PLANNED_SCREENS`, which no longer holds the workers screen; the workers page test asserts that `/app/workers` no longer says "it shows no data" and that the navigation entry opens the built screen. `style-nonce.test.tsx` renders `App` directly and gains the `workers` prop, a genuine change of its props, as 31b's `turnRecords` did.

## Departures

- **Reachability, capacity, and last contact (plan Q2 (b)).** Row 33 of `docs/g3-plan.md` names "reachability and capacity" for this item, and the POC shows them. `WorkersView` carries no reachability, capacity, last contact, draining, admission pressure, harness, or credential generation per worker (the wire contracts spec even refuses an entry with a credential generation); the brief and the coordinator keep the wire unchanged. The screen shows what the wire carries: the admission and self-check results. The contract change is a follow-up (below); the row's wording is left to the coordinator.
- **Revoked workers (Q7 (a)).** `WorkersView` lists "every enrolled worker that is not revoked", and revocation is a CLI command without a web operation; the screen says nothing of revoked workers, and the POC's "Revoked" badge has no source.

## Follow-ups

- **Contract change for reachability, capacity, and last contact**, an OpenAPI and control-plane change outside this item's web scope: per-worker `reachable` with `lastContactAt`, declared and reserved slots, CPU, and memory, draining, and admission pressure with its cause on `WorkerAdmissionEntry` or a view of its own, so the screen can show the POC's badges and meters.
- **A worker-changed event** on `streamProjectEvents` or an installation stream, so the screen can follow the view live instead of a snapshot with Refresh (Q5 (b) and (c) were not chosen).
- **The enrollment tokens (33b)** add their section and the link of the empty state; **the account slots (33c)** add theirs; both extend `WorkersGateway`.
- **Rename of the board gateway's name "board"** for the session reads it carries (31a note) stays a follow-up; this change adds no read to it.

## Proof of concept

The screen follows `docs/ui-poc/src/screens/Workers.tsx` (`WorkersScreen`, the "Machines" section of `WorkerCard`s): one card per worker with the hostname as its heading, the platform line, and a role rule that names `installation-owner` ("Needs <role>" of `RoleButton`, `docs/ui-poc/README.md` "Roles"). Departures, each with its requirement, decision, or the reason below ("Reference proof of concept"):

- **No reachability, draining, revocation, or admission pressure badges, no slot, CPU, or memory `Meter`s, no "Last contact" or "credential generation", no harness badge, and no Drain or Resume.** The wire carries none of them (departure above; wire contracts "Workers view operation"), and no worker command exists on the wire; a departure recorded against the plan row, with the contract follow-up.
- **No "Enroll worker" button and no enrollment steps.** Enrollment tokens are 33b (brief); the empty state names the token without a link (Q4 (a)).
- **Self-check results and the admission state are shown, which the POC lacks.** Product UI "Automatic worker admission": the failed checks are shown on the workers screen (Q3 (a), Q7 (a)).
- **No header count badges.** The counts came from reachability (departure above). The cards keep the POC's responsive `SimpleGrid` (pass 1 finding 8 restored it; the first revision used a single-column `Stack` without an authority for the departure).
- **The whole page is gated, not only actions.** The read itself needs `installation-owner` (Q2 (a)); the POC gated only the Enroll, Drain, and Resume actions, since it showed fixture data.

## Risks and trade-offs

- The session's roles are those of the last successful session read, which `SessionProvider` makes at the announced end of the session and on `refresh`; between reads, an operator whose role was granted sees the gate, and one whose role was revoked sees the control plane's 403. The control plane is the authority either way (decision 4).
- A second Refresh while one is in flight sends a second read; the first answer is dropped by the effect's cleanup, so the newer read wins whichever answers first. No in-flight guard was added: the reads are idempotent and the screen never shows an older answer over a newer one.
- `failedFirst` reorders a worker's checks on the screen, so the check identifier order of the wire is kept only within each group; a test pins both the order and the grouping.
- The `not-found` texts of the page are unreachable through the adapter (decision 7); they exist because the shared `ReadFailure` carries the kind, and the page test covers them through the fake gateway.

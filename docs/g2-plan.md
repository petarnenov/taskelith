# G2 work plan

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: in progress. G2 started on 2026-10-25, when the G1 closure ([ADR 0010](decisions/0010-g1-closure.md)) merged into `dev`, as the owner decided. On 2026-10-25 the owner decided D1–D14 below, each by accepting the author's recommendation, and asked for the added dependencies of items 14 and 15 on the pre-G2 contracts. By D14 (a), items start in dependency order and are merged without a per-item approval, except an item blocked by an owner decision added later and still open. Item 9 added the first G2 runtime workspaces; the archived changes under `openspec/changes/archive/` record each completed item.

G2, "Durable execution vertical slice", is defined in [architecture section 13](architecture.md#delivery-sequence): PostgreSQL state and outbox, NATS transport, enrollment and authorization boundaries, a local worker journal, a supervisor, and a deterministic test harness; running the real worker protocol even on one host; a minimal local installation composition, the `EvidenceStore`, launch authorization, and resource admission. Its exit evidence: submit an admitted task, assign it, execute it, and persist its result, and exercise duplicate delivery, process-launch crash windows, partition, cancellation races, stale generations, and disk loss. Section 13 requires each gate to be decomposed through the SDLC framework into reviewed, independently verifiable work items; this plan lists them. Each item is one OpenSpec change, developed in its own git worktree and merged into `dev`.

## Starting point

- G1 delivered the private workspace packages `@taskelith/kernel` (opaque identifiers, failure vocabulary, typed results, instants, event metadata), `@taskelith/sdlc` in `packages/modules/sdlc` (workflow model, gates with compare-and-set generations, task admission with the `TaskAdmissionService`, `AdmissionRepository` and `Clock` ports and an in-memory repository, the `SdlcFramework` port and its conformance suite), `@taskelith/plugin-sdk` (manifest schema and library-level registration), `@taskelith/contracts` (`sdlc.asyncapi.json` and `sdlc.openapi.json`, generated types and validators, and the breaking-change check in `make verify`), and, from G1 item 10, the plugin packages `@taskelith/sdlc-openspec` and the fixture-only `@taskelith/sdlc-speckit`. The specifications are the `sdlc-*`, `shared-kernel`, `plugin-registration`, `contract-tooling`, and `workspace-packages` capabilities in `openspec/specs/`.
- G2 builds on the port frozen by [ADR 0009](decisions/0009-frozen-sdlc-framework-port.md) and on the G1 closure, [ADR 0010](decisions/0010-g1-closure.md), including its list of the pre-G2 contracts.
- G1 item 7 deferred the ownership generation of an admission to G2, and G1 decision D4 (a) deferred the attempt and assignment state machines to G2.
- [ADR 0007](decisions/0007-g0-closure.md) deferred two items to G2: implementing the data namespace of the [storage contract](storage.md) (identity and ownership checks, root locks, atomic persistence, crash consistency, credential storage), and starting native acceptance of the [worker matrix](worker-platforms.md), including qualification of the harnesses on Linux inside the worker image.
- [Architecture section 15](architecture.md#15-decisions-still-requiring-evidence-or-owner-scope-confirmation) requires launch linearization and expiry, supervisor identity and adoption, local journal durability, and capacity reservations to become reviewed protocol contracts before G2 execution code. The fifth pre-G2 contract, both-framework mapping feasibility, is G1's work (items 10 and 12) and is confirmed by `close-g1`.
- The [trust contract](trust-model.md) and the [operational targets](operational-targets.md) fix the identities, enrollment, credential generations, launch authorization TTL and skew (`30s`/`5s`), resource budgets, journal backlog limits, disk floor, and broker retention that G2 implements and tests.
- The root manifest already lists the `packages/adapters/*` and `packages/plugins/*` workspaces, where the adapters of items 11, 12, 14, 15, and 19 belong, although no package exists under `packages/adapters/` yet (item 11 creates the first); only `apps/*` is missing. `make verify` already needs Docker for the OpenAPI breaking-change check.
- PostgreSQL, NATS JetStream, and every runtime client library remain `candidate` in the [dependency register](dependencies.md), with no version or image selected.

## Recorded limitations

Item 27 lists these among the closure's deferrals.

- **Rotated worker credentials.** `rotate-worker` issues a new credential generation, but G2 has no path that delivers it to the worker, and the worker cannot adopt it. After the grace window the worker loses access, and the only recovery is a new worker identity with a new token and root, after the old identity is revoked and its attempts are reconciled ([installation and enrollment contract](contracts/installation-and-enrollment.md) section 6). Delivery belongs to G3 item 26. The owner's delegated decision-maker decided this on 2026-10-25, while the owner was away.
